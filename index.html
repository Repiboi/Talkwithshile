GOOD TO KNOW

A few things worth knowing.

Need more information? Create an enquiry and ask about any item in the collection.

Can I pay for products on this website? +

Not yet. This site is a product catalogue and enquiry tool, not an online checkout. Prices, availability and payment arrangements should be confirmed directly with the business.

Where can I find product ingredients and usage advice? +

Add the product to your enquiry list and request its full ingredient list, usage instructions, contraindications and other precautions. For medical concerns, consult a licensed healthcare professional.

Will my enquiry list stay saved? +

Yes. Your selected products are saved in this browser on this device. They are not uploaded to a server.

Does this website offer medical treatment? +

No. Product listings are informational and are not a diagnosis, treatment recommendation or guarantee of results. Do not replace professional medical care with products on this site.
